# SMAP Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

SMAP (Sky-MusaAutoPlay) is an open-source Android application maintained by LingYunALingYun. This policy explains how the application handles information.

## Information stored on your device

SMAP stores imported and downloaded scores, favorites, playlists, playback settings, language and theme preferences, instrument pitch settings, calibrated key positions, and your Accessibility disclosure choice on your device. Android may include this data in system backups according to your device and Google account backup settings. You can remove local data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it.

## Cloud library and optional account login

SMAP connects to `https://musetreehouse.com`, a service operated by the same developer, to browse and download public scores and cover images. Network requests necessarily disclose technical information such as your IP address to that server, and the server may retain security or access logs. The service is hosted on Alibaba Cloud, which processes data as an infrastructure service provider on the developer's behalf.

If you voluntarily sign in to a Muse Tree House account, SMAP sends the username and password you enter to the developer-operated service over HTTPS for authentication. The Android application does not store your password. After a successful login, the application stores the returned account ID, username, MID, and avatar URL locally so it can display your account state. Signing out removes this locally stored account information. Muse Tree House account data and server-side deletion requests are handled through that service.

## Accessibility and overlay features

Accessibility access is optional and is used only after you enable Game Mode. It dispatches taps at the key positions you calibrate so that a selected score can be performed in Sky. SMAP does not use Accessibility to read screen content and does not collect, store, or share personal or sensitive data through the Accessibility API. You may decline this permission and continue using normal playback, or disable it at any time in Android settings.

The optional display-over-other-apps permission and foreground service are used to show and operate the Game Mode floating control. They are not used to collect data.

## Data sharing and security

SMAP contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Data is sent only when required for the optional Muse Tree House cloud features described above. Network access to that service uses HTTPS. Links to GitHub, GNU GPL, and Muse Tree House open in your browser and are then governed by those services' policies.

## Children

SMAP is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. The optional cloud account service may have its own eligibility requirements.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this repository with a revised effective date. To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of account data, email:

lingyuna.ritsu@gmail.com

For non-sensitive technical questions, you may also open an issue at:

https://github.com/lingyunalingyun/Sky-MusaAutoPlay-SMAP-Android/issues

# SMAP Android 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 21 日

SMAP（Sky-MusaAutoPlay）是由 LingYunALingYun 维护的开源 Android 应用。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

## 设备本地保存的信息

SMAP 会在设备本地保存导入和下载的曲谱、收藏、播放列表、播放设置、语言与主题、乐器音高、琴键校准位置以及无障碍用途说明的同意状态。Android 可能按照设备和 Google 账号的备份设置将这些数据纳入系统备份。你可以通过清除应用数据或卸载应用删除本地数据。

## 云曲库和可选账号登录

SMAP 会连接由同一开发者运营的 `https://musetreehouse.com`，用于浏览和下载公开曲谱及封面。网络请求会向该服务器提供建立连接所必需的 IP 地址等技术信息，服务器也可能保留安全或访问日志。该服务部署在阿里云，阿里云作为基础设施服务提供商代表开发者处理相关数据。

如果你主动登录缪斯树屋账号，SMAP 会通过 HTTPS 将你输入的用户名和密码发送给开发者运营的服务进行验证。Android 应用不保存密码。登录成功后，应用会在本地保存服务返回的账号 ID、用户名、MID 和头像地址，以显示登录状态；退出账号会删除这些本地账号信息。缪斯树屋账号资料及服务器端删除请求通过该服务处理。

## 无障碍和悬浮窗功能

无障碍权限是可选的，并且只在你开启游戏模式后使用。该功能按照你校准的琴键位置发送点击，以便在《光·遇》中演奏所选曲谱。SMAP 不通过无障碍 API 读取屏幕内容，也不通过该 API 收集、存储或分享个人及敏感数据。你可以拒绝授权并继续使用普通播放，也可以随时在 Android 设置中关闭权限。

可选的“显示在其他应用上层”权限和前台服务仅用于显示及运行游戏模式悬浮控制，不用于收集数据。

## 数据分享和安全

SMAP 不包含广告或分析 SDK。仅在使用上述可选缪斯树屋云功能时发送必要数据，与该服务的网络通信使用 HTTPS。应用内的 GitHub、GNU GPL 和缪斯树屋链接会在浏览器中打开，之后受对应服务的政策约束。

## 儿童隐私

SMAP 并非专门面向儿童，也不会明知而收集儿童个人信息。可选云账号服务可能具有其自身的使用资格要求。

## 政策变更与联系

重大变更会在本仓库中发布并更新生效日期。如需咨询隐私问题，或申请访问、删除账号数据，请发送邮件至：

lingyuna.ritsu@gmail.com

非敏感的普通技术问题也可以在以下页面提交 Issue：

https://github.com/lingyunalingyun/Sky-MusaAutoPlay-SMAP-Android/issues
